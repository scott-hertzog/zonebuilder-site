About

Built from years of scattered musical knowledge.

ZoneBuilder grew out of decades of notebooks, lesson notes, binders, spreadsheets, PDFs, tabs, recordings, videos, bookmarks, and half-remembered practice plans.

The goal is simple: help musicians organize both practice and musical knowledge into a structured system that supports real growth.
